# Project Pulse — Frontend (project-pulse-system)

## Stack
- Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS
- Backend: Laravel + Sanctum (token-based auth), running separately at project-pulse-backend
- Database: PostgreSQL (owned by the backend, not this repo)

## Roles
Dean, Student, Adviser, Panelist, Grammarian, Dean's Secretary (the database role name; the website's internal key is "Secretary" and its pages are under `/secretary`). It was called "Research Coordinator" until 2026-11-30, when the school's adviser said that is not the real title: **never write "Research Coordinator" or "Coordinator" again, write "Dean's Secretary"** (migration `rename_research_coordinator_to_deans_secretary`)

## How a student gets in (no approval step)
- **A student registers and is Active at once** (`POST /register`; UB email `7 digits@ub.edu.ph`, the Terms box is required). The Dean's Secretary no longer approves students: `StudentApprovalController`, the `/student-approvals` routes, the "Student Approvals" page and the "waiting for approval" notice are gone. The migration `students_active_without_approval` let in anyone still Pending. The Dean can still disable (or re-enable) any account on the Users page.
- After logging in a student with no accepted group lands on **`/student/setup`** ("Pending Group Assignment"): it checks by itself every 30 seconds, has a Refresh button and a Log out button. When an adviser adds the student to a group (the adviser's student list takes any Active student not in an open group), the page shows the invitation; accepting opens the dashboard.
- **Known risk:** the email is not verified (nobody proves the student owns that UB address), so someone could register with another person's number. The adviser is the only gate, because they choose whom to add; a student with no group sees nothing. Email verification of the UB address is the proper fix if it becomes a problem.

## Module 2 rules (Groups, Projects, Title proposals) — set by the school's actual process
- **Group size:** 1 to 3 students (the leader plus up to 2 members). Enforced in `GroupController::MAX_MEMBERS` and
  `_utils/studentSelection.ts`.
- **Stages (own column `projects.proposal_stage`, only these 4):** Title Proposal -> Dean's Endorsement -> Client
  Consultation -> Ready for Proposal Defense. There is no free stage dropdown; each move is one explicit action
  (`ProposalStageController`): Adviser "send to Dean" -> Dean "endorse" (the sign-off itself advances the stage) ->
  Adviser "choose title". Approving a title never moves the stage.
- **Titles:** only the group's Adviser approves / requests revision / rejects. The Dean has NO title-approval action
  (do not add one). Up to 3 titles Pending or Approved at a time. Approving does not set the project title; the single
  chosen title is set at "choose title" and must be one the Dean endorsed.
- **Every title proposal records which student sent it** (`title_submissions.submitted_by`, shown as "Submitted by <name> · <date>, <time>" (Philippine time, `formatDateTime`) to the group, the adviser and the Dean; the adviser's "New title proposal" notice also names the student). Proposals sent before this existed have no name and just say "Sent <date>": nobody guesses. The one exception is a backfill migration (`backfill_title_sender_for_solo_groups`): a group that has only ever had ONE student (one member, nobody ever left) can only have been written by that student, so those old proposals got the name; groups of 2 or 3 stay empty. Final outputs (Dean's Secretary Group Folders / Collection Tracker / Archive Records, `OutputList`) also show "Submitted by <name> · date, time".
- **Title proposal lists are closed rows** (`CollapsibleTitle`: title, "Submitted by <name> · date, time", status, "Open ▼"), for the student, the adviser (the Approve / Request revision / Reject buttons sit inside the opened row) and the Dean (both the endorsement list and the project's title list). A group with many revisions or rejections must stay short: never show every proposal's content open at once.
- **A title proposal is written in a simple text editor** (`ProposalEditor`, TipTap: bold, italic, underline, bullet and numbered lists only; a Title box above it). The student writes
  everything in it (context, target audience, technology stack...): there are no separate fields, and nothing is pre-filled. It is stored as editor JSON in
  `title_submissions.proposal_content` (never HTML), checked by the manuscript whitelist (`ManuscriptContent::check`, max 200 KB), with its plain text copied to
  `project_description`. `api/projects/*/titles` is exempt from `TrimStrings`/`ConvertEmptyStringsToNull` like the manuscript. `ProposalView` shows it read-only to the student,
  adviser and Dean (older proposals with only plain text still show). Keep this page simple: do not add structured fields to it.
- **The letter for interview is a form on the Forms shelf, not a feature** (the user's decision, 2026-12): the Dean or the adviser uploads the school's letter once in Forms and the students download it. The system does NOT draft, attach, edit, print or check it (the old `InterviewLetterController`, `App\Support\InterviewLetter`, the student editor, the Dean's viewer and the letter PDF were removed; the table `interview_letters` is left in the database untouched). `send-to-dean` needs no letter. The only thing left is a **reminder**: when at least one title is approved the adviser presses "Remind the group" (`POST /groups/{id}/letter-reminder`, `LetterReminderController`, Adviser only, once an hour per group) and the students get a notice (in the app and by email) to do the letter, pointing them to the Forms tab; the student's Title Proposal screen also shows a plain "Next step" line once a title is approved. Do not rebuild the letter editor unless asked.
- **Dean's endorsement:** records the endorsed (approved) titles, notes, signer and time (`dean_endorsements`); never the
  chosen title.
- **Return to Adviser:** at Dean's Endorsement the Dean can send the group back (`POST /groups/{id}/return-to-adviser`) with a required
  note (5+ characters). The stage goes back to Title Proposal; the note (`projects.dean_return_note`) is shown to the adviser and the
  group until she sends the group to the Dean again, which clears it. The Dean still decides nothing about titles. While the group is
  at Title Proposal the adviser may change an earlier approval to "Needs Revision" or "Rejected" (never back to Approved), which frees
  room under the 3-title limit.
- **Leaving / removal:** a student can leave; the Adviser or Dean can remove one, but a reason category is required and goes
  to the audit log. Leaving moves the person to `group_member_exits` (history kept). If the leader leaves, the member who
  joined earliest becomes leader. If nobody is left the group becomes 'Pending Closure' until the Adviser/Dean confirms; it is
  never deleted. After the Dean's endorsement the Adviser's and Dean's normal Remove is locked, but **a student can always leave on their own (the Leave button stays open, the endorsement stays); only the Dean's separate exception removal (reason +
  details, Critical audit entry) can change it.
- **Closing a group:** a student is in at most one group that is not `Closed` (`Project::currentFor` is the one "current project" rule).
  A group that lost every member is confirmed closed by its Adviser or the Dean. The Dean alone can also close a group that still has
  members (`POST /groups/{id}/close` with `reason_category`, plus `reason` for "Other"): for a failed group that will retake, a stopped
  project, or a disbanded group. That cancels its scheduled defenses, tells the students, adviser and panel, keeps all history, stores
  `student_groups.closed_reason` and writes a Critical audit entry. Finished/archived groups are deliberately NOT closed automatically:
  every student screen skips Closed groups, so closing them would hide a graduate's own project from them.
  The Dean can undo a close (`POST /groups/{id}/reopen`, reason required, Critical audit entry): the group goes back to Active with its
  members, project and history; defenses cancelled by the close stay cancelled. Refused if the group has no members or a member has
  since joined another open group.
- **Changing advisers:** one group via `PUT /groups/{id}/adviser`, or all of an adviser's open groups (or chosen `group_ids`) via
  `POST /advisers/{id}/reassign-groups` (Dean only; the source may be a disabled adviser, the target must be Active; Closed groups never
  move). Both use `Project::assignAdviser`. An adviser's own assignment applies to the groups they advise now; work a group already handed
  in for it stays with the group (`Requirement::appliesTo` + `Project::hasHandedIn`) and the new adviser can review it, but cannot edit
  or delete the previous adviser's assignment. Assignments the group had not started do not follow it.
- **School term view:** the Dean's Projects and Group Management lists open on the current term (`_lib/terms.ts`: August = 1st semester,
  January = 2nd, June = summer), with a term select ("All terms" to see everything). The "needs your attention" list is never filtered by term.
- **Concurrency:** title proposals and decisions, stage moves, submissions, reviews, receipts, and defense scheduling, evaluation, result and
  cancel all lock their rows (group, then project, then the item) and re-check the rule inside the lock. A review may send `version_no`
  and is refused if a newer version was uploaded meanwhile (`StaffRequirements` sends it).
- **Defense order is strict, no skipping:** Proposal -> Alpha -> Beta -> Omega -> Final (`Defense::TYPES`). A type can be scheduled only after the group
  passed (Passed or Passed with Revisions) the one before it (`Defense::previousType` / `hasPassed`, checked inside the project lock in
  `DefenseController::store`). A failed defense is defended again; a cancelled one counts as not held.
- **Defenses are scheduled on a sheet, many groups at once** (`/adviser/defense/schedule`, `/dean/defense/schedule`, `ScheduleSheet` + `SheetGrid`; the board's "Schedule defense" button opens it, there is no pop-up for new
  schedules any more, so the sidebar toggle stays usable). One row per group of the chosen defense type: "Fill the schedule" gives the ticked groups consecutive slots (`_lib/scheduleSheet.ts`: `autoFillSlots` skips the lunch break,
  never runs past the end of the day, moves to the next allowed weekday), every cell is editable (**each group picks its own Start and End from the time that is still free: `availableStarts` / `availableEnds` in `_lib/scheduleSheet.ts` hide what another row or an existing defense already uses in the same room or with the same panelist, and the length is worked out from them, there is no minutes box**; the quick fill asks only for the first day, the start and end time (lunch break under "Lunch break"), the venue and the panel: `planSchedule` chooses the length (45 minutes, or 30 when that is what keeps every group on the first day) and the following days (Monday to Saturday) by itself; Enter moves down, fill-down for venue/panel, pasting date/time/venue rows from Google Sheets or Excel is read by `parseDateCell` /
  `parseTimeCell`), and a room or panelist booked twice is flagged while typing (`findIssues`, also checked on the server). The second tab is the same sheet for the scheduled defenses (edit, "Save changes", cancel, "Print / Save as PDF").
  Server: `GET /defenses/eligible?type=` (`DefenseSchedule::eligibility`: the strict order, already scheduled/passed, not active, archived), `GET /defenses/busy`, `POST /defenses/batch` and `PUT /defenses/batch` (every row checked, all saved or
  none, `row_errors` keyed by project id / defense id, one audit entry, panelists get ONE notice for the batch). A defense has a length (`defenses.duration_minutes`, default 60); no room or panelist may be booked twice at overlapping times
  (`DefenseSchedule::conflicts`, also for single scheduling), and scheduling takes `pg_advisory_xact_lock` so two people cannot take one slot. **State updaters must be pure** (they run twice in development) and a late load must never replace
  what the user typed (`latest`/`touched` refs in `ScheduleSheet`). Students read their adviser's whole schedule, read-only, on `/student/defense` (`StudentSchedule`, `GET /defenses/schedule`: no results, scores or remarks of anyone).
- **Who records a defense result:** for the **Proposal Defense** the group's **Adviser** records it (the group is still hers; the panelists' evaluations and the "ready for its result" notice go to her; the Dean cannot, except for a group with no adviser). For **Alpha, Beta, Omega and Final** the **Dean** records it and is notified (`DefenseController::mayRecordResult`, route `PUT /defenses/{id}/result` is Adviser,Dean; the board shows the other role a plain note).
- **The Proposal Defense is scored on the department rubric** (`App\Support\ProposalRubric`, `_lib/rubric.ts`, `RubricPaper`, panelist page `/panelist/rubric/[id]`, `RubricDialog` for the Dean and adviser, PDF `_lib/manuscriptExport/rubric.ts`): ten criteria in five sections, each rated 4 Excellent / 3 Good / 2 Fair / 1 Needs Improvement, total out of 40, the same criteria for every course (only the course name in the title changes, taken from the group). The panelist clicks one box per criterion on a copy of the paper form; `PUT /defenses/{id}/evaluation` then needs `ratings` (every criterion) instead of a typed score, and `evaluations.score` is the percentage worked out from them (so averages and the Dean's result screen keep working). The ratings are in `evaluations.rubric`; the panelist's signature picture (`user_signatures`, now also for Panelists) is copied onto the evaluation when it is saved (`evaluations.signature`), so a signed form never changes; `GET /defenses/{id}/rubric/{panelistId}` (that panelist, the adviser, the Dean) returns the form with the signature, which is never in the lists. The other defenses keep the plain 0 to 100 score until their own rubrics are given. Defenses list the group's `members` for staff and panelists (not students).
- **Module boundaries:** after "Ready for Proposal Defense", Module 5 (Defenses), Module 3 (Requirements) and Module 7
  (Records) track their own progress in their own fields. A Proposal Defense can only be scheduled for a Ready group.
  Requirement `stage` labels live in `Requirement::STAGES` / `REQUIREMENT_STAGES`, separate from the proposal stages.

## Assignments (Requirements page) — Title Proposal and sections
- **Title Proposal is the first assignment**, not a separate page. Each adviser automatically gets one built-in
  `requirements` row (`kind = 'Title Proposal'`, created by `Requirement::titleProposalFor`). The student proposes titles and the
  adviser decides on them inside the Requirements page (`StudentTitleProposals`, `AdviserTitleReview`). The Module 2 rules
  above still apply unchanged. It cannot be deleted; the adviser may only edit its instructions, due date and sections. The
  Dean's Requirements list leaves it out (the Dean uses the endorsement queue).
- **Who sent a submission and when is always shown, with the time:** "Submitted by <name> · Nov 23, 2026, 2:15 PM" (`formatDateTime`, Philippine time) on the student's Requirements page, the adviser/Dean group rows (visible without opening the row), every line of the version history (`RequirementSubmissionVersion::shape` returns `submitted_by`) and the Documents page for every role. Use `formatDateTime`, not `formatDate`, for moments people sent or saved something; `formatDate` is for due dates.
- **Instructions:** every assignment has "Instructions for students" (`requirements.description`), shown to students as "From <adviser>".
- **Sections:** an assignment can be limited to chosen sections (`requirements.sections`, labels like "BSIT 4A", NULL = all of the
  adviser's groups). The dropdown lists the sections the adviser actually advises. `Requirement::appliesTo` enforces it.
- **The Requirements page for Adviser and Dean is a drill-down, one clean screen at a time** (`RequirementsExplorer`, path shown across the top: Courses › BSIT › 4A › Chapter 1): all five courses (`COURSE_OPTIONS`: BSCS, BSIS, BSIT, BLIS, ACT) -> that course's sections -> the assignments of the section (Title Proposal first for the adviser) -> the groups of one assignment. Structure comes from `GET /groups` and each assignment's `/requirements/{id}/submissions` rows (so an assignment limited to "BSIT 4A" only appears under that section). Groups are closed rows (name, status, "Open ▼"); a group's submission, version history and review buttons show only after clicking it (`SubmissionRow`; Title Proposal: `AdviserTitleReview` with `compact`). Inside an assignment the filter row (`GroupFilterBar`, `compact` = Status + name search only) shows with 2+ groups. The Dean's Secretary and Grammarian keep the flat queue (`FlatRequirements`). New assignment screens must follow this and never show every submission already open.
- **Forms shelf** (a "Forms" tab beside Assignments on the Dean and Adviser Requirements page, and beside Assignments / Pending / Completed on the student page; `FormsShelf`, `SharedFormController`, table `shared_forms`, `GET|POST /forms`, `DELETE /forms/{id}`, `GET /forms/{id}/file`, suite `shared_forms_smoke.php`): the user's own idea, kept deliberately plain like a shared drive. The Dean or an adviser uploads a file once (name, optional note, PDF/Word/Excel/PowerPoint/picture/ZIP, 10 MB); students download it. **No stages and no "shows up when": do not add them unless asked.** Each row has **View** (eye: PDF, picture or Word read in a pop-up, never as a web page) and, for a student and a Word (.docx) form, **Edit** (pencil): `POST /my-documents` makes a new extra document of the group titled like the form and `/student/manuscript/{id}?fromForm={formId}` copies the form text in once through `importWordFile` (the form on the shelf never changes; the copy is then an ordinary group document, shareable with the adviser). Files that cannot be shown (Excel, PowerPoint, ZIP) keep a plain Download button. Adviser, Dean and the three read-only roles only View. The Dean's forms reach every student, adviser, Panelist, Grammarian and Dean's Secretary, today and for every later batch (read only for the last three, each has a "Forms" sidebar page); an adviser's reach that adviser's students only (and no other adviser sees them); the Dean sees all. Only the uploader or the Dean removes one (Warning audit). The letter for interview is just a form on this shelf (see Module 2 rules).
- There is no `/adviser/title-proposals` page any more (the old address redirects to Requirements).
- **File rules per assignment** (`allowed_types`, `max_file_mb` 1-100 (`Requirement::MAX_FILE_MB`; the host's php.ini `upload_max_filesize` / `post_max_size` must be at least the biggest size chosen, default new assignment 25 MB), `allow_resubmission`): enforced by `Requirement::fileRules()` on
  upload. With resubmission off, a new version is only accepted after the adviser asked for changes.
- **Template/guide:** the adviser or Dean can attach up to 3 files to an assignment (table `requirement_attachments`, `Requirement::ATTACHMENT_LIMIT`, 25 MB each, `/requirements/{id}/attachment[/{attachmentId}]`, the old single-file columns were emptied by a migration); students of the targeted sections, the adviser and
  the Dean can download it (`/requirements/{id}/attachment`).
- **Word (.docx) preview and "Download everything (ZIP)" on the Documents page:** `Requirement::PREVIEW_TYPES` now includes `docx`. A Word file is read in the browser (`DocxPreview`, `mammoth` through `importWordFile`) and drawn by the same read-only editor as manuscripts (`ManuscriptView` takes HTML text or JSON), so only the editor schema's content is shown, never raw HTML; fonts and exact layout of the original are not shown (a note says so, Download gives the real file). Old `.doc` files are download-only. `GET /projects/{id}/documents.zip` (`DocumentController::archive`, Student own group, Adviser advised groups, Dean, Dean's Secretary) streams the current file of every submission the person may open (each passes `RequirementAccess::canViewSubmission`, so the Dean's Secretary only gets cleared final outputs) as `<assignment> (version N) - <file>`, unsafe characters replaced, links in `links.txt`, 404 when there is nothing; it uses `maennchen/zipstream-php` (pure PHP, no temp files, works with the cloud bucket, does not need `ext-zip`). The page's ZIP link shows for a student, and for staff once one group is chosen in the new group filter (or the search leaves one). - **Documents page** (`/student|adviser|dean/documents`, `DocumentLibrary`): the current file or link of every submission the role may see
  (`GET /documents`, `DocumentController`), with version history and an in-browser preview for PDF and images. The preview forces

## Manuscript (built-in rich-text document) — `/student/manuscript`
- One manuscript per project, written in the browser (`ManuscriptEditor`, TipTap). It is stored as the editor's JSON (`manuscripts.draft`),
  **never HTML**, and rendered only by the editor itself (`ManuscriptView`, read-only). Do not use `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` for it.
- **Manuscript home and extra documents (Step 1, the user's Google Docs / Hermes idea)**: `/student/manuscript` now opens a HOME (`ManuscriptHome`): "Blank document", the **Official manuscript** card (links to `/student/manuscript/official`, the editor described below, unchanged) and the group's **Other documents** (`/student/manuscript/[id]`, `DocumentEditor`). Extra documents (`group_documents`, `GroupDocumentController`, `GET|POST /my-documents`, `GET|PUT|DELETE /my-documents/{id}`, `PUT /my-documents/{id}/sharing`, suite `group_documents_smoke.php`) use the same editor and whitelist (`ManuscriptContent::check`, `api/my-documents/*` is exempt from trimming) and, since Step 1.5, the SAME live co-editing as the official manuscript (Yjs over `CollabProvider` with `basePath: /my-documents/{id}`, `GroupDocumentCollabController`, table `group_document_collab_updates` + `group_documents.collab_seq`, routes `POST /my-documents/{id}/collab/sync|start|compact` and `PUT /my-documents/{id}/snapshot`, suite `group_document_collab_smoke.php`; the readable copy in `group_documents.content` is what the adviser/Dean read when shared). A rename (`PUT /my-documents/{id}` with only `title`) needs no `base_revision`; text sent that way still needs it (old path, still tested). No versions, no checkpoints, no activity for them. **They are private to the group unless a member ticks the adviser and/or the Dean in the Share dialog** (read only; `GET /projects/{id}/shared-documents`, `GET /shared-documents/{id}`, shown by `SharedDocuments` above the versions in `ManuscriptHistory showActivity`; a notice is sent when shared). **The official manuscript is never private** (the Dean's daily-monitoring wish). Max 50 extra documents per group. **Step 2, review of an extra document** (`GroupDocumentReviewController`, tables `group_document_reviews` / `group_document_comments` / `group_document_comment_replies`, routes `POST /my-documents/{id}/reviews`, `GET /documents/{id}/reviews`, `GET /document-reviews/{id}`, `POST /document-reviews/{id}/comments`, `PUT /document-reviews/{id}/decision`, `POST /document-comments/{id}/replies`, `PUT /document-comments/{id}/status`, suite `group_document_review_smoke.php`): a member presses "Reviews" > "Send for review" (the document must be shared first; the editor saves its readable copy first), which freezes a copy (copy 1, 2...). The adviser and the Dean (only for documents shared with their role; unsharing closes the door) comment on words or the whole copy, the students reply, adviser/Dean resolve. **Only the Adviser decides Approved / Needs Changes** (note of 5+ characters for changes), same as the official manuscript. It moves no stage. The screen is the SAME `ManuscriptReview` as the official manuscript, given a `source` adapter (`ReviewSource`); `DocumentReviews` is the button + list (student editor and `SharedDocuments`). Not built (ideas): panelists/Grammarian reading extra documents, version history for them.
- **Draft** is private to the group (only students can read or save it) and is **edited live by all members at once** (see Live co-editing below).
  The old `PUT /my-manuscript/draft` (with `base_revision`, 409 on a newer one) still exists and is still tested, but the editor no longer uses it.
- **Live co-editing** (the group types in the same document, like Google Docs; `CollabProvider` in `_lib/collab.ts`, `ManuscriptCollabController`, tables `manuscript_collab_updates` +
  `manuscripts.draft_collab_seq`, suite `manuscript_collab_smoke.php`): the document lives in each member's browser as a **Yjs CRDT** (TipTap `Collaboration` + `CollaborationCaret`, which replaces
  the editor's own undo, so Undo only takes back MY typing). There is **no WebSocket server**: the browser calls `POST /my-manuscript/collab/sync` about once a second (every 5 s in a hidden
  tab, sooner while typing) with its new change messages (base64, merged with `Y.mergeUpdates`) and its cursor, and gets everyone else's messages after its `cursor` id plus who is online (cursors are
  short-lived cache entries, 25 s). The server never reads the messages: it stores them in order (every write takes the manuscript row lock so ids commit in order and nobody can skip one), so two people typing
  at once merge instead of overwriting; nothing is lost offline (the queue is kept and retried). The first member to open a manuscript turns the saved `draft` JSON into the starting Yjs document
  (`POST /collab/start`, accepted for one member only, so the starting text is never doubled). `POST /collab/compact` lets a browser that is fully in step replace the log with one merged copy
  (after 150 messages). **The readable copy**: each member's browser saves the editor JSON a moment after ITS OWN typing stops (`PUT /my-manuscript/snapshot {content, seq}`, whitelist-checked by
  `ManuscriptContent::check` like every other save, ignored as `stale` if `seq` is lower than the one kept, then retried); that JSON is what Submit version, the adviser, the Dean, search and exports read, so a
  bad message can only upset the group that sent it. "Submit version" first syncs, saves a fresh copy and submits (retries on 409 if another member saved between). Restoring an old version replaces the draft
  **for everyone editing** (the confirmation says so). Who edited since the last version is recorded when a member sends changes. The routes have their own limiter (`throttle:collab`, 600 a minute per person)
  and do not use the normal 120 a minute. Latency is about 1 to 2 seconds, not instant; if instant typing is ever needed only the transport (`CollabProvider`) has to be swapped for WebSockets (Reverb). The screen
  shows "<names> is also editing now", coloured cursors with names, and "No connection" while offline. Read-only views (versions, review, preview) build the editor WITHOUT the collaboration extensions.
- **Versions** (`manuscript_versions`) are frozen copies made by "Submit version": who saved it, when, an optional note, word count, and every
  member who edited since the previous version. They are never changed. The adviser, the Dean and the group read versions
  (`GET /projects/{id}/manuscript/versions[/{no}]`, `ManuscriptHistory`, shown on the Dean's project profile and the adviser's group card).
- **What the server keeps is a whitelist** (`App\Support\ManuscriptContent`): node types, marks and each attribute's value pattern. Pasted papers
  carry things that cannot be kept safely (pictures on other sites, `#_Toc` contents links, Word style values): the server removes only those,
  counts them (`dropped` in the save response, plus the cleaned `content` so the editor shows what is really saved) and saves the rest. Only a
  non-document, an unknown block type, or an oversized/too deep document is refused (422). If the editor gains a new extension or attribute, add it
  to the whitelist or it is silently dropped (`manuscript_smoke.php` has a document captured from a real session). Pictures are stored inside the
  document as data URLs (shrunk client-side to under 1 MB each); pasted pictures that are not data URLs are dropped and the student is told.
- A scrolling bubble beside the scrollbar shows the page on screen ("7 of 70") (`PagedScroll`).
- Defaults are Arial 11 pt, 1.5 lines of spacing (Word's meaning, see below), 1 inch margins; students can change fonts, sizes and spacing like Google Docs, and their captions,
  titles and references are their own business (the system does not check them).
- **Pages** (`pagination.ts`, a ProseMirror plugin): Letter size, 1 inch margins. The sheet is one element; the plugin draws grey
  gaps and top margins as display-only widgets and moves content that would cross a boundary to the next page (paragraphs split line by
  line; tables, lists and pictures move whole). Nothing is stored for pages, so the same count shows in the editor and in frozen versions.
  The sheet is at least 816 px wide (it scrolls sideways on a phone) so a page is always the same shape.
- **Never trim or null-convert manuscript requests**: `bootstrap/app.php` exempts `api/my-manuscript/*` from `TrimStrings` and
  `ConvertEmptyStringsToNull`, because spaces between bold and plain text are real content (this once produced "broken text block").
- **Compare versions** (`ManuscriptCompare`, engine in `_lib/manuscriptDiff.ts`, tests in `tests/manuscriptDiff.test.cjs`): any two submitted versions,
  older on the left. Nothing is stored: both frozen documents are fetched and compared in the browser, first paragraph by paragraph
  (aligned like a text diff), then word by word inside a paragraph that was rewritten (a replaced pair counts as one edit when enough words
  survive; pictures are never "edited"). Formatting is ignored on purpose; only words count. Green underline = added, red strikethrough =
  removed. "Show only changes" folds unchanged runs, Previous/Next walk the changes. The dialogs render through a portal to `document.body`.

  and the Dean select words in a frozen version (or comment on the whole version); the students read and reply; only the adviser/Dean mark a comment
  Resolved or reopen it (reopening notifies the group). A comment lives on a **version**, never on the draft, so it keeps pointing at the same words.
  Its place is `block_index` (the n-th block of `toBlocks()` in `_lib/manuscriptDiff.ts`) plus character offsets inside that block's text, with the quoted
  words kept as a fallback: **do not change `toBlocks()` without a migration plan for stored comments.** The version list shows open/resolved counts and a

  `selectionchange` (not the mouse), and the "+ Comment" button acts on pointer-down so a tap cannot clear the selection first.
- **Export to PDF and Word** (`ManuscriptExportDialog`, `_lib/manuscriptExport/`: `model.ts` reads the saved JSON into simple blocks, `pdf.ts` = pdfmake,

  letter widths, files in `public/fonts/export/`, fetched on export); Calibri, Cambria, Georgia and Verdana fall back to those in the PDF only. The Word file keeps the font names, uses
  Heading 1-3 styles, real lists and a real table-of-contents field (Word asks to update fields on opening: answer Yes). Not opened in real Word during development, only inspected.
- **Line spacing means what it means in Word**: "1.5" is 1.5 lines, and a line of Arial is about 1.15 times its size, so the editor draws `line-height: calc(1.5 * 1.15)`

## People with more than one role (role selector)
- One account, one email, one password; a person has a main role (`users.role_id`) and may hold extra ones (`user_roles`). **Only Adviser, Panelist and Grammarian can be held together** (`User::COMBINABLE_ROLES`,
  checked in `UserController::roleProblem`; Student and Dean's Secretary always have exactly one). **A Dean may also act as Panelist, and nothing else** (`User::DEAN_EXTRA_ROLES`: the Dean sometimes sits on a defense panel; Dean is only ever the main role, never an extra one). A Dean may give THEMSELVES the Panelist role on the Users page (still never change their own main role or status); acting as Panelist, the Dean has no Dean powers (the token's role decides). Nothing stops a Dean who is on a panel from also recording that defense's result as Dean. The Dean ticks "Also acts as" on the Users page (`extra_role_ids` on `POST|PUT /users`).
- A person acts as ONE role at a time: the chosen view is kept on the sign-in token as the ability `role:<name>` (`ApplyActiveRole` middleware sets `$user->actingAs`, and `User::getRelationValue('role')` returns it), so every
  `$user->role->role_name` check, `role:` route middleware and query works unchanged. A token with no chosen role, or for a role no longer held, acts as the main role. Login opens on the last view used (`users.last_role_id`),
  `PUT /me/role {role}` switches (audit entry), login and `/me` return `roles`. The web app keeps `roles` in localStorage (`getStoredUserRoles`) and `ProfileSwitcher` makes each role its own **profile**: the name beside the picture in the top bar shows "<Role> profile ▾" for a person with 2+ roles and opens a "Switch profile" list (one row per role, the current one marked); choosing one does a full page load into that role's dashboard.

- **Looking people up by role must use `User::holdingRole('Adviser')`** (main OR extra role), never `whereHas('role', ...)` or `where('role_id', ...)`; `Notifier::withRole`, the panelist lists and the adviser checks do. ## Passwords and security
- **Self-service:** `/forgot-password` asks for the email and `POST /forgot-password` always answers the same (no way to learn which emails exist); an active account gets an email with a one-time link (60 minutes, token hashed in
  `password_reset_tokens`) to `/reset-password?token=&email=` on the web app (`FRONTEND_URL` in the backend `.env` decides the address). The person chooses the password there (`AuthSplit`, `PasswordStrength`); every sign-in of theirs ends.
  Signed-in people change it on their profile (`ChangePassword`, `PUT /me/password`, needs the current one, other devices are signed out).
- **The Dean never types or sees a password:** `POST /users` takes no password (random unknown one + an invitation email, `AccountInvitation`); the old `reset-password` route is gone; the Users page only has "Email link"
  (`POST /users/{id}/password-link`, audit Warning). Changing a person's email signs them out. `php artisan dean:create` (the installer's own command line) still sets the first Dean's password.
- **One rule everywhere** (`AppSupportPasswordRules`, `_lib/password.ts`): 8+ characters, upper and lower case, a number, a special character.
- **Mail:** `MAIL_MAILER=log` writes emails to `storage/logs/laravel.log` (fine on a laptop). A live server needs real SMTP settings in the backend `.env` (`MAIL_*`, `MAIL_FROM_ADDRESS`), otherwise nobody receives a link.
- **Email copies of notifications** (`App\Support\Notifier::email`, `App\Mail\NotificationMail`, view `resources/views/mail/notification.blade.php`): every in-app notice is also emailed to the active person (title, the same words, a button to the page it opens, built from `FRONTEND_URLS`). Chat messages are not emailed, nor the person who caused the notice. A mail failure is logged and never breaks the action. It runs inline (`QUEUE_CONNECTION=sync`); on a live server set a real mailer and, ideally, a queue worker. UAT accounts use `@uat.test`, which cannot receive mail.
- **Terms and Privacy** (`/terms`, `/privacy`, `LegalPage`, public pages; text drafted by us, to be checked by the school): students tick "I agree" on registration (`accept_terms` required by `POST /register`); everyone else agrees by choosing a password from the emailed link. `users.terms_accepted_at` records when. The faculty invitation email says "Confirm my account" and opens `/reset-password?...&welcome=1` ("Confirm your account", then goes to login).
- **Session safety in the browser** (`_components/SessionGuard.tsx`, mounted once in the root layout): **every Log out button must call `requestLogout()`** (a confirmation, never an immediate log out); the browser's Back button on a portal page that would land on the login/landing page is undone and asks the same question (the popstate listener is registered at module load so it runs before Next.js's own; do not move it into a component effect, Next would navigate first); 30 minutes with no mouse/key/scroll/touch (shared across tabs through localStorage) shows "Are you still there?" for the last minute and then signs out (`/login?expired=1` explains why); signing out in one tab signs out the others (`storage` event); a portal page restored from the browser's memory after a log out goes to /login. `IDLE_MS` is at the top of the file. The login page also tells a signed-in visitor "You are still signed in" (direct visit).
- **Other safeguards:** sign-in locks for 15 minutes after 10 wrong passwords for one email from any computer (on top of 5 a minute per email + computer); password emails are limited (5 an hour per email + computer);
  tokens expire after 8 hours and are ended on disable, password change and email change; API answers carry security headers (`SecurityHeaders`: nosniff, frame deny, no-store, CSP none) and the web app too (`next.config.ts`: frame deny, nosniff,
  referrer, permissions, HSTS). Not added on purpose: a page-wide Content-Security-Policy (needs careful testing with the editor, PDF previews and exports).

## API conventions
- All API calls go through `apiFetch<T>(path, options)` in `src/app/_lib/api.ts`
- It automatically attaches the Bearer token from storage
- On a 401 response, it auto-redirects to /login
